# LumaFlow Pitch & Jury Defense

## Problem Statement
Automated trading primitives like Stop-Loss and Limit orders are essential for institutional and retail DeFi. On Stellar Soroban, traders are currently forced to rely on centralized custodial bots.

## Solution
LumaFlow introduces native, slippage-bounded automation to Stellar Layer 1 with non-custodial escrow and open keeper bounties.
